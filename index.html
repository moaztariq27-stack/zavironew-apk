Install ZAVIRO Web App (PWA)

ZAVIRO runs as a full-screen mobile app on both Android and iPhone (iOS) with live Firebase sync.

🤖 On Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet)
  1. Tap the "Install App" button on the top banner, or open browser menu (⋮).
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Launch ZAVIRO from your home screen in full-screen standalone mode!
🍎 On iPhone / iPad (iOS Safari)
  1. Open ZAVIRO in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow pointing up ⬆️) at the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap "Add".
  4. Open ZAVIRO from your iPhone Home Screen for a native full-screen experience!
